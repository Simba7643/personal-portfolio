Who I am

I'm a recent B.Sc. graduate in Electrical & Computer Engineering (Computer Engineering stream) from Debre Berhan University, Ethiopia. My coursework spans software engineering, digital systems, computer architecture, and networks — and my project work has pushed that further into cybersecurity and applied machine learning.

During my internship at INSA (Ethiopian Cyber Security Institute), I worked hands-on with penetration testing, which shaped how I think about building systems: assume they'll be attacked, and design monitoring in from day one.

I code with AI. It's part of nearly every project below, from an AI summarizer to a full campus social platform.

I'm now looking for a role where I can grow as a network, systems, or support engineer — and eventually, build my own ventures.
